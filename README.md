# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing and understanding *Customer Churn* (the rate at which customers stop doing business with an entity). Using this interactive *Power BI Dashboard*, businesses can easily identify high-risk customer segments, uncover the core drivers of customer attrition, and derive data-driven insights to optimize customer retention strategies.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools Used
* *Power BI Desktop:* Used for data modeling, advanced visualization, and building the interactive user interface.
* *Power Query:* Utilized for data cleaning, handling missing values, profiling, and ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) processes.
* *DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):* Used to write customized calculations, time-intelligence functions, and advanced business metrics (e.g., Active Customers, Churn Rate %, Revenue at Risk).
* *Excel / CSV:* Served as the primary source dataset.

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Analyzed
The dashboard actively monitors and tracks the following essential business metrics:
* *Total Customers:* The overall size of the active customer base.
* *Churn Rate (%):* The percentage of total customers who have canceled their subscriptions or services.
* *Lost Customers:* The total count of customers who have churned.
* *Revenue At Risk:* The total monetary loss incurred due to customer attrition.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights
Following a comprehensive data exploration, these critical insights were discovered:
1. *Contract Type:* Customers on *Month-to-Month* contracts exhibit a significantly higher churn rate compared to those on 1-year or 2-year commitments.
2. *Tenure (Customer Lifetime):* New customers (tenure under 6 months) sit heavily in the high-risk zone, indicating friction during onboarding.
3. *Payment Method:* A disproportionately high churn percentage was observed among customers utilizing Electronic Checks as their primary payment method.
4. *Tech Support:* Customers who did not opt for or receive Technical Support services demonstrated a much higher tendency to leave the platform.

---

## 🎯 Actionable Recommendations
* *Proactive Onboarding:* Implement targeted email flows or loyalty discounts during the first 6 months to reduce early-stage tenure churn.
* *Contract Migration:* Incentivize Month-to-Month subscribers to upgrade to longer-term (1 or 2-year) contracts by offering attractive bundle pricing.
* *Payment Optimization:* Encourage a transition from Electronic Checks to secure automated payment options like Credit Card or Direct Bank Autopay.
* *Support Outreach:* Run a targeted campaign offering free technical check-ups or feature walkthroughs for users who haven't interacted with tech support.
